# Code of conduct

This project adopts the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(v2.1), shortened to what actually applies here.

## Expected

- Assume competence and good faith. Most disagreements here are about data
  quality or licensing, not about people.
- Critique the data, not the contributor: "this coordinate is 400 m off" is
  useful; "you clearly don't know Rome" is not.
- Ask before assuming: country-level heritage registers differ wildly, and
  someone editing from Ljubljana knows things a maintainer in Dublin does not.
- Respect licences and attribution even when it is inconvenient. That is the
  whole product.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, insults, or sustained disruption in issues, pull requests or
  discussions.
- Removing or obscuring author attribution, licence metadata, or source links.
- Submitting data or code whose provenance you cannot state.
- Using the project to scrape services whose terms forbid it.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer ([@thepeternemec](https://github.com/thepeternemec))
or through GitHub's report abuse form. Reports are read by the maintainer only.

Responses escalate: a clarifying comment, then a warning, then a temporary block.
Spam and scrapers get blocked immediately. There is no appeals committee; there
is a maintainer who will explain the decision.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere the project
is represented. Off-project behaviour is out of scope unless it makes
collaboration here unsafe.
